The bot studies what the top 100 traders on Hyperliquid with earnings of $10k–$500k are doing. It learns from their trades and applies the same logic to ETH and SOL, calculating 34 technical indicators simultaneously. It automatically enters trades when the signal is strong enough, dynamically manages risk, and stops itself if things go wrong."

What This Bot Does – Complete Overview

🔍 Module 1 – Smart Money Scanner
Scans the Hyperliquid public leaderboard and filters 100 profitable traders with:

PnL between $10,000 and $500,000 (no lucky millionaires, consistent performers only)
Win rate ≥ 52%
Minimum 50 trades (statistically significant)

For each trader it extracts:

Average leverage used
Margin type preference (isolated vs cross)
Average trade duration
Most traded assets
Win rate on longs vs shorts

This builds an aggregate smart money profile that the bot uses to calibrate its own behavior. If profitable traders average 6x leverage, the bot won't exceed 9x even on a perfect signal.
Results are cached in smart_money.json so it doesn't re-scan every time.

📡 Module 2 – Technical Analysis Engine (34 indicators)
Every 60 seconds, the bot fetches live candle data for ETH and SOL and calculates:
Trend indicators:

EMA 9 / 21 / 50 / 200 – direction and cross detection
ADX + DI+ / DI- – trend strength (above 25 = real trend)
Parabolic SAR – dynamic trend reversal points
Ichimoku Cloud – trend, support/resistance, momentum combined

Momentum indicators:

RSI 14 – overbought/oversold with bounce detection
MACD 12/26/9 – momentum and cross signals
Stochastic RSI – faster momentum on extreme levels
CCI – statistical deviation from mean price
Williams %R – fast overbought/oversold
MFI (Money Flow Index) – RSI weighted by volume

Volume indicators:

Volume MA 20 – spike detection (> 1.5× average)
OBV (On Balance Volume) – cumulative buy/sell pressure
VWAP – fair value anchor, directional bias

Volatility / Structure:

Bollinger Bands 20,2 – mean reversion levels
Keltner Channels – volatility-adjusted bands
Donchian Channels – breakout detection
ATR 14 – current market volatility (used for SL/TP sizing)

Perpetual-specific (Hyperliquid only):

Funding Rate – market sentiment, overcrowded positions
Open Interest – capital flow into the market
Mark Price vs Index Price – premium/discount to spot


🧠 Module 3 – Signal Scoring System
Every indicator has a weight. After checking all conditions, the bot produces two scores:
LONG score:   0–100
SHORT score:  0–100
Example breakdown for a LONG signal:
✅ EMA bullish cross          +15 pts
✅ Trend UP (EMA9>21>50)      +10 pts
✅ ADX > 25, DI+ > DI-         +8 pts
✅ RSI bounce from oversold   +12 pts
✅ MACD bullish cross         +10 pts
✅ Volume spike 2.1×           +7 pts
✅ Confirmed on 4H             +10 pts
❌ Not near BB Lower            +0 pts
─────────────────────────────────────
   Total LONG score:  72/100
A trade is only opened when score ≥ 45. Below that, the bot does nothing.
